RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items must be unused, undamaged, and returned with original packaging and accessories. Some hygiene and customized products may not be eligible for return.

To request a return, sign in to your account, open Orders, select the delivered order, and choose Request Return. The support team reviews the request and may ask for photographs when an item is damaged or incorrect.

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers may take 5 to 10 business days to show the credit after the refund is processed. Cancelled orders that have already been paid are eligible for a refund review.

For a damaged or incorrect item, report the issue within 48 hours of delivery and attach clear photographs. Keep the product and packaging until the support team responds.
