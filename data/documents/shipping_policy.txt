SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY

Orders are normally processed within 1 to 2 business days. Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch, depending on the destination and courier service.

Customers can view shipment progress from the Orders page using the tracking number. A tracking page may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show the first scan.

If a shipment has not moved for more than 3 business days, contact customer support with the order number. Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by weather, public holidays, address issues, or courier delays.

Please ensure the shipping address and phone number are correct before placing an order. Address changes may not be possible after dispatch.
